Sources and interpretation

Current rates checked 13 November 2026 against Anthropic pricing and OpenAI pricing. Each historical table row separates rate evidence from effective-date evidence. A current price page alone does not establish a historical price or its start date. Unverified points remain provisional, including assumptions that rates stayed constant between observations.

Best / Standard / Cheap are editorial groupings of selected model families, not benchmark rankings or equivalent capabilities. Families can change model, tokenizer and price. Percentage changes include these substitutions and, in AUD, FX changes. The chart is not a cost-per-task or productivity comparison, nor an exhaustive model catalogue.

Before caching is recorded as available, hypothetical cache-read volume is charged at the ordinary input rate. Labels may group multiple releases with the same rates; they do not imply all named models existed on the first date.
